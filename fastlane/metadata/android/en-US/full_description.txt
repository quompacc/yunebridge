YuneBridge is a fork of <a href="https://codeberg.org/Freeyourgadget/Gadgetbridge">Gadgetbridge</a> with a redesigned, modern interface. It talks to your smartwatch, fitness band or earbuds directly – without the vendor's app, without a cloud and without an account.

All functions of Gadgetbridge are kept, including:
<ul>
<li>Activity, sleep, heart rate and other health data, stored only on your phone</li>
<li>Notifications, calls and music control on the watch</li>
<li>Installing watch faces, apps and firmware files</li>
<li>Alarms, weather and many device-specific settings</li>
<li>Data export and backup</li>
</ul>

YuneBridge can be installed next to Gadgetbridge. Device support, protocols and background services come unchanged from Gadgetbridge; YuneBridge only changes the look.
